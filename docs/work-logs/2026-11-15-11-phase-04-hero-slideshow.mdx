## Objective and final implementation

User requested a separate hero component, subtle bottom decoration and five-second slideshow, then clarified the original overlapping two-image arrangement and full-screen-width section without a card enclosure. Further requested dot indicators, no pause behavior/control and removal of the club discovery subtitle.

GuestHero.tsx and guest-hero.css isolate hero state/styles from GuestLanding.tsx. Four existing assets rotate: campus illustration, SINNO, HBAC and technology photograph. Initial large campus/small technology pair matches the original composition. Every five seconds the current large image transforms into the small lower-left frame and the next image moves into the large frame. The section spans the viewport with no outer border/radius, while content stays aligned with page columns. Thin curved lines decorate the lower edge. Dot buttons select slides with aria-pressed and accessible names. No pause state, hover/focus stop or pause control remains, per explicit user instruction. Reduced-motion CSS removes animated transitions while retaining requested automatic cycling.

Removed the club discovery subtitle and retained the right-aligned search label. Reused local assets without adding dependencies or modifying backend/database. GuestHero.test.tsx covers exact five-second timing, four-image looping, large-to-small ownership, manual dot selection and continued cycling on hover.
